## [0.1.1] - 2026-07-15

### Changed

- **Navbar** is now a full-width bar whose content sits in `.navbar__container`,
  mirroring `.container`'s max-widths so it lines up with page content instead of
  spanning the full viewport. Add `.navbar--fluid` to opt back into full width.
- **Navbar links** use a subtle brand-color hover instead of a background
  highlight.
